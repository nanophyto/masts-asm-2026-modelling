Getting started

On a workshop Cloud Workstation

Open the individual launch link provided by your instructor and sign in with your assigned nanophyto.org account. The link opens the participant workshop/ folder directly, with a short README, the OceanBioME exercises, Abil.py material and a results/ folder.

In the File Explorer, open 01_OceanBioME/00_reference_column.jl and choose Julia: Execute File in REPL from the Command Palette (Ctrl+Shift+P). The first REPL launch may compile its editor integration; keep it open as you move between exercises. Run 01_OceanBioME/01_grazing.jl next to explore biological responses. Saved PNGs appear in results/oceanbiome/ and open with a click in Explorer. Use Ctrl+B and Ctrl+Shift+E to restore the Explorer if it was hidden in a previous session.

Scientific walkthrough

The reference LOBSTER column introduces Oceananigans and OceanBioME model construction, coupling and simulation, with plots of vertical ecology and export. The grazing experiment compares two biological parameter choices. The physics appendix explains the prepared light and mixing conditions used by both exercises.

For local installations, Python and Julia setup, and independent figures, follow Running the examples on your own system.
